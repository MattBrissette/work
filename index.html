OatFi 01 / 06

B2B, Fintech
Contribution: Web Design & UX User Map Information Architecture Email Design Design System Illustration Brand Evolution Marketing Assets

OatFi is a fintech lender focused on B2B payments, offering API infrastructure that lets platforms embed working capital tools directly into payment flows.

As OatFi exited stealth mode, I helped shape its positioning as a modern alternative to traditional lenders, contributing to a bold, dynamic brand built around black, white, and neon accents. I worked on the visual identity and modular design system, creating an adaptable language that reflected how OatFi’s API integrates across different platforms and industries.

For the website, I led the UX strategy, information architecture, and design—creating custom icons, custom illustrations, and brand guidelines to deliver a simple and engaging experience that clearly communicates OatFi’s product, differentiation, and value through bold visuals, natural language, and purposeful microinteractions.

Result

“The design team created a compelling website and built out a client success playbook, both of which help us stand out among the competition. They were able to take complex technical concepts and distill them down to the core value propositions that will contribute to OatFi's explosive growth.”

— Mike Barbosa, CEO, OatFi

Terraformation 04 / 06

B2B, Climate Tech
Contribution: Web Design & UX Landing Page Content Design Marketing Assets

Terraformation works to solve the biggest bottlenecks in native forest restoration. To launch the Seed to Forest Alliance, it needed a digital presence that combined ecological credibility with modern, technology-forward design.

I led UX, information architecture and visual design for Terraformation's digital experience and the Seed to Forest Alliance platform, establishing a cinematic art direction. That included a research-driven story on why seed supply matters, an event page for NYC Climate Week, and a sign-up form for partners, landowners and carbon buyers.

I also designed marketing ads and campaign assets for the NYC Climate Week event, extending the cinematic visual language across digital and social touchpoints.

MCIM 05 / 06

B2B SaaS

PE-backed tech company Fulcrum Collaborations was growing fast. As awareness expanded for both the corporate brand and its flagship SaaS product, MCIM (Mission Critical Information Management), it became clear that future growth would be amplified by the power of one cohesive brand. TrailerTrader 06 / 06

B2B/B2C Marketplace
Contribution: Dashboard Design Web Design & UX E-Commerce Mobile-First Design System Lead Generation

As TrailerTrader became the leading marketplace in its category, its existing product needed to evolve to support increased traffic, modern buyer expectations, and a more sophisticated dealer ecosystem.

A major hurdle for many buyers was knowing they needed a trailer but feeling overwhelmed by technical specs and unsure of which category suited their needs. To solve this, we introduced a guided recommendation tool that lets shoppers answer a few quick questions about their haul, towing capacity, and usage, instantly pointing them to the ideal trailer type.

I created a comprehensive design system, designed a new dashboard for dealers, and delivered a faster, mobile-first experience with simpler navigation and stronger lead generation for dealers. This created more direct connections between buyers and dealers while providing a scalable foundation for future feature releases.

A spatial search engine built for discovery, not scrolling. As design engineer, I owned this end to end, concept, interaction design, and the full Three.js/WebGL build — turning search results into an explorable 3D environment where clusters and unexpected connections emerge instead of a ranked list.

The experience pulls live data from DuckDuckGo, Wikipedia, OpenAlex, Hacker News, The Met, and the Art Institute of Chicago. Users orbit through the space, inspect nodes, expand a result into a new search, pin discoveries into a personal constellation, and export findings as Markdown notes.

Built from scratch with Three.js, WebGL, GLSL, GSAP, live APIs, spatial algorithms, and GPU instancing, with AI-assisted development (Gemini + Claude) supports thousands of entities while staying real-time and interactive.

About

I'm an Interactive and Digital Experience Designer with 10 years of experience, based in the Gatineau–Ottawa region. I design websites, e-commerce, email, and interactive experiences for B2B technology companies, turning complex products into clear, engaging stories.

I work from UX, information architecture, and wireframes through to polished design, within an established brand or helping evolve it. I enjoy working collaboratively in teams—partnering closely with developers, strategists, and founders to take projects from early ideas to build-ready execution.

I use GenAI and 3D/WebGL to explore ideas faster and go beyond standard templates. My work has been recognized by Awwwards and CSS Design Awards. I work in English and French.
