B2B, Medtech

RocketAir 03 / 08

Design & Technology
Contribution: Web Design & UX Creative Strategy Editorial Typography Art Direction Motion Concept Brand Identity

A digital experience created in collaboration with RocketAir, blending creative strategy, thoughtful design, and high-impact visuals into a modern, engaging web experience.

Visuals were inspired by editorial/brutalist, bold typography as the visual anchor. A lo-fi space theme is contrasted against a bright neon yellow accent, set against a high-contrast, near-monochrome black-and-white base.

The whole experience was designed with motion in mind, with content sitting at the corners and edges rather than centered, leaving generous negative space to breathe.

OatFi 07 / 08

GeoStories 08 / 08

Campaign, Social Media
Contribution: Strategy Design lead Art direction

Young Canadians are passionate about experiential travel and sharing memorable moments on Instagram. We identified Canada's most frequently geotagged locations, researched hundreds of location-specific stories, and created a system for the team to manually reply with relevant historical facts whenever users tagged one of those destinations.

We also created Instagram accounts for key figures from the Geological Survey’s long history, allowing users to explore the stories behind the facts they received. Each geologist’s profile linked to an updated Wikipedia page, giving users an easy way to learn more and continue their discovery.

Lab

A spatial search engine built for discovery, not scrolling. As design engineer, I owned this end to end, concept, interaction design, and the full Three.js/WebGL build — turning search results into an explorable 3D environment where clusters and unexpected connections emerge instead of a ranked list.

The experience pulls live data from DuckDuckGo, Wikipedia, OpenAlex, Hacker News, The Met, and the Art Institute of Chicago. Users orbit through the space, inspect nodes, expand a result into a new search, pin discoveries into a personal constellation, and export findings as Markdown notes.

Built from scratch with Three.js, WebGL, GLSL, GSAP, live APIs, spatial algorithms, and GPU instancing, with AI-assisted development (Gemini + Claude) supports thousands of entities while staying real-time and interactive.

About

I design brand identities, websites, e-commerce, marketing campaigns, social media assets, print collateral, and interactive experiences for B2B technology companies—connecting brand and demand to turn complex products into clear, engaging stories.
