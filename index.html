Lab

Re-Aktor

RE-AKTOR is an experimental WebGL experience inspired by Eastern European industrial architecture, post-Soviet infrastructure, and the atmosphere of an abandoned cooling silo. Through procedural particles, atmospheric lighting, generative textures, and reactive sound, it transforms a fictional decommissioned silo into a living, cinematic environment.

I led the visual direction, composed the soundtrack, and developed the experience through an AI-assisted creative coding workflow. From shaders and particle simulations to environmental rendering and interactive audio, RE-AKTOR explores how design, sound, and technology can come together beyond traditional interface conventions.

mattbrissette.github.io/re-aktor

Habs Penthouse

HABS Penthouse is an interactive 3D concept celebrating the Montreal Canadiens, reimagining a modern luxury lounge built around the team's identity. The experience combines architectural details, custom hockey-inspired elements, a simulated live broadcast, collectibles and cinematic lighting to create a space that feels like an extension of the game itself.

Built with Three.js and AI-assisted creative coding, the project turns a static interior into an explorable experience. Users can rotate the room, switch camera angles, adjust materials and lighting, interact with objects and even trigger stadium-inspired ambience, blending 3D visualization, interface design and playful interactivity.

mattbrissette.github.io/habs-penthouse

About

I'm a Visual and Digital Designer with 10 years of experience creating engaging brand and digital experiences across campaigns, websites, e-commerce, and digital products. I combine strong visual design and art direction with a user-focused approach, translating creative ideas, audience needs, and business objectives into compelling visual stories and digital experiences.

My work spans creative direction, visual design, UI/UX, interaction design, wireframing, and prototyping. I enjoy taking projects from initial concept through to execution, collaborating with cross-functional teams to develop thoughtful experiences that balance creative ambition, usability, and business impact.

I'm also passionate about exploring new technologies and creative approaches. I integrate AI tools like ChatGPT and Claude into my workflow to accelerate ideation, experimentation, and prototyping, alongside hands-on experience in 3D, WebGL, motion, and interactive experiences.

My work has been recognized by Awwwards, CSS Design Awards, Applied Arts, WARC, and Behance. Fluent in both English and French.
